# Example: Historical Storytelling

> Generic illustration of Profile C. Specific historical claims below are illustrative scaffolding; a real episode must verify every detail against sources.

## Premise
A short story about a market town waiting for a delayed river barge during a cold autumn in a pre-electric era (candle and oil lamp). Only this premise is depicted.

## Period checklist (illustrative, pre-electric European market town)
- Buildings: timber-framed and stone, thatch or slate roofs, cobbled square
- Lighting: candle, oil lamp, lantern; **no electric light**
- Clothing: wool cloaks, linen shirts, leather boots, simple caps
- Transport: carts, barrels, barges; no engines
- Not yet existing: plastic, wires, modern glass, brand logos, zips

## Bible highlights
- Palette: base `#1D2024`, mid `#43494B`, warm highlight `#C8B98E`, accent `#7A5C2E`, neutral `#8A8F8C`.
- Locations: `loc_market_square` (variants: `dawn_mist`, `dusk_lantern`), `loc_river_quay`.
- Characters: anonymous townspeople as `group`, silhouettes only (no real figures).

## Scene breakdown (40 s)
| Scene | Time | Narration (abridged) | Shot / movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| s01 | 0-6 | The town woke to fog on the river. | Establishing wide, foreground arch, slow push-in |
| s02 | 6-13 | By noon the barge still had not come. | Medium crowd silhouettes at the quay, slow pan |
| s03 | 13-20 | Merchants counted barrels twice. | Insert, hands on barrel rim, practical light |
| s04 | 20-30 | Dusk brought lanterns to the square. | Wide, `dusk_lantern` variant, parallax fog |
| s05 | 30-40 | The river stayed empty. | Held static wide, empty quay, dip to black |

## Sample prompt (s01)
`Wide establishing view of a pre-electric market town square at dawn, timber-framed houses and stone arch in foreground, cobblestones wet with mist, townspeople as distant silhouettes in wool cloaks, soft diffuse daylight, muted grey-brown palette with warm highlights, subtle grain, historically accurate, no modern objects.`
Negative: `anachronism, modern buildings, plastic, zippers, wires, fantasy armour, neon, text overlays`

## Notes
Faces of real historical figures are never invented. When uncertain, the shot becomes a silhouette, a detail insert or an environmental view.
