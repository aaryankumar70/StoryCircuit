# Example: Mystery / Investigative

> Generic illustration of Profile B. The scenario is fictional and uses no real people or events.

## Premise
A short investigative story about a lighthouse logbook whose last page is missing. The narrator follows the clue from a sealed archive box to a faded harbour map. Only these elements are used.

## Bible highlights
- Profile: `mystery_investigative`; palette: ink `#10151A`, slate `#26323A`, steel `#4B6270`, paper `#CFC6AE`, **amber clue** `#B8873B`.
- Characters: none on screen except a silhouetted archivist (`char_archivist`, `presentation: silhouette_only`).
- Locations: `loc_archive_room` (steel shelving, one desk lamp), `loc_harbour_map_table` (large map under a lamp).
- Motifs: torn page edge (macro), pool of lamp light on paper.

## Scene breakdown (30 s)
| Scene | Time | Narration (abridged) | Shot / movement | Reveal beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| s01 | 0-4 | One page was missing. | Insert, macro of torn page edge, static | Tease |
| s02 | 4-10 | The logbook sat in a sealed archive box. | Establishing wide, slow push-in, one lamp | Orientation |
| s03 | 10-16 | The last entry was dated... | Top-down desk, slow pan across the page; amber light on the date | Confirm |
| s04 | 16-23 | A harbour map had a pencilled mark. | Rack focus from page to map | Clue connection |
| s05 | 23-30 | Someone else had looked at it first. | Silhouette archivist at shelf, back-lit, static | Consequence |

## Sample prompt (s03, insert)
`Top-down view of an old lighthouse logbook open on a wooden desk, torn page edge visible, handwritten date partially legible with illegible-by-design smudged text, single warm desk lamp pooling amber light on the date, cold slate surround, paper-toned page, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain.`
Negative: `readable fake names, logos, gibberish text, trench coat cliche, conspiracy wall`

## Notes
Text on pages is deliberately smudged to avoid fabricating "evidence". One amber accent per frame teaches the viewer where to look. Reveals use tease -> confirm -> consequence.
