# Banned patterns (why scripts read as "AI")

The machine-checkable list is `resources/banned_phrases.json`. Beyond that, avoid:

1. **Throat-clearing openers**: greetings, "today we", topic announcements.
2. **Hype adjectives without proof**: shocking, incredible, mind-blowing, fascinating. Show the fact instead.
3. **Fake cliffhangers**: "But here's the thing", "But wait", "Plot twist". Just state the twist.
4. **Moral summary endings**: "This serves as a reminder that..." End on a concrete image or the hook echo.
5. **Rhetorical question stacking**: max one question in the whole script.
6. **Triplets everywhere**: "fast, smart, and bold" patterns repeated. Vary rhythm.
7. **Vague attribution**: "experts say", "some believe", "many people". Name the source or drop the claim.
8. **Padding**: restating what was just said, summarising before the end.
9. **Uniform sentence length**: mix 4-word punches with 12-16 word sentences.
10. **Em-dash and semicolon heavy prose**: spoken language does not use them.
11. **Every sentence starting with the same word** (He... He... He...).
12. **Science/biology framing** of any kind (see niche policy).
